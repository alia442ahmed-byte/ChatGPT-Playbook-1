Do This Before Anything Else NEW
Stop ChatGPT From Storing Your Business Secrets
Your pricing. Your clients. Your process. Keep all of it private.
⚠️ Why Every Business Owner Needs to Know This

By default, OpenAI may use what you type to train their AI. That means your pricing, your client lists, your job notes could end up in their training data. Turn this off in 3 steps before you type anything private.

1
Open Settings
In ChatGPT, click your profile picture in the bottom-left corner. Then click Settings.
2
Find "Data Controls"
Inside Settings, click on Data Controls. This is where ChatGPT's privacy options live.
3
Switch Off "Improve the model for everyone"
You'll see a toggle with that label. Turn it OFF. That's it. ChatGPT will no longer use your chats for training. Your conversations stay yours.
✓
Turn Off Training
Settings → Data Controls → Toggle OFF
✓
Use Fake Names
Say "my customer" not their real name
✓
Keep Work in Projects
Separate your business chats (Step 05)
💡 Smart Rule for Your Whole Team

Tell anyone on your team who uses ChatGPT: Never type a real customer name, address, phone number, or price sheet you want to keep secret. Use "Customer A" or "our standard rate" instead. It's a small habit that protects you.

Phase 2
Build Your Master Prompt
Stop explaining your business every time. Upload it once. It works forever.
1
What Is a Master Prompt?
Think of it like a travel agent who knows everything about you — where you like to sit on the plane, when you like to travel, all your preferences. A Master Prompt does that for your business. It tells ChatGPT your services, your prices, your ideal client, your goals. Once it's built, ChatGPT already knows who you are. No more starting from scratch every single chat.
2
Let ChatGPT Build It for You
Don't write it yourself. Open a new ChatGPT chat, hit the voice button (microphone icon), and speak your answers out loud, like talking to a new hire. Answer honestly and in detail. Use the prompt below to get started.
📋 Copy & Paste This Into ChatGPT
I am the owner of my business and I want to build a "Master Prompt" — a document that tells you everything about who I am and how my business works so I never have to explain it again. Act as an elite business consultant. Ask me one question at a time about: 1. My business model — what I sell, how I price it, how I make money 2. My ideal customer — who they are, what they struggle with, what they want 3. My team and how the business runs day to day 4. My biggest time wasters and frustrations right now 5. My top 3 business goals this year After I answer everything, write it into one clean document I can save as a PDF and reuse. Ready? Ask me question #1.
3
Save It as a PDF
Once ChatGPT builds your document, copy the text and save it as a PDF. Name it something like My_Business_Master_Prompt.pdf. Upload it to your Projects in Step 05. From that point on, every ChatGPT chat in that project already knows your business: your voice, your prices, your goals. Nothing repeated.
⚡ What This Unlocks

With your Master Prompt saved, you can ask things like: "Write a follow-up email to a customer who got an AC quote 3 days ago." ChatGPT already knows your tone, your offer, your pricing. It writes a usable email on the first try.

Phase 3
Set Your Voice & Style
Make ChatGPT sound like you, not like a robot writing a school essay.
1
Find Custom Instructions
On a desktop, click your profile photo (bottom-left) → Settings → Personalization → Custom Instructions. You'll see two boxes to fill out.
2
Box #1 — Tell ChatGPT Who You Are
Copy and customize this for your first box:
📋 Box #1 — About Me
I own a business. I'm the decision-maker. I value speed, clarity, and real-world results over theory. I'm not a tech person. Keep things simple and direct. Give me answers I can act on today, not explanations I have to decode.
3
Box #2 — Tell ChatGPT How to Talk to You
This stops ChatGPT from giving you long, fluffy, robotic answers. Copy this:
📋 Box #2 — How to Respond
- Keep answers short and easy to scan. - Use bullet points and bold text. No long essays. - Write like you're talking to a smart, busy business owner. - Never say "Certainly!", "Great question!", or "I hope this helps." - Give me the answer first. Explain after if needed. - If you're missing info, ask me one clear question. Don't guess.
🔥 Before vs. After

Before: ChatGPT writes a 500-word essay with a fluffy introduction and three disclaimers.
After: ChatGPT gives you a clean, direct, bulleted answer in 10 seconds. Like a sharp employee who already knows you.

Phase 4
The Canvas Tool
Edit documents with ChatGPT side by side. Like Google Docs with a genius assistant built in.
1
What Is Canvas?
Canvas opens a live document next to your chat. You write something, ChatGPT improves it. You click on a sentence and change it. You go back and forth until it's perfect. Think of it like having an editor sitting right next to you.
2
How to Open It
At the top of any chat, look for the Canvas button. Or just type: "Open this in Canvas." The document editor pops up on the right side of your screen.
3
Build a Template — Then Lock It In
Ask ChatGPT to draft a proposal, a sales email, an employee checklist, or an onboarding doc in Canvas. Refine it until it's exactly right. Then type:

"Write a prompt that would create this exact document every time — with the same format, tone, and structure."

Save that prompt. That is your business's intellectual property. The future value of your business will be in your prompts. Now any team member can produce the same quality output in seconds, without you involved.
💡 Real Business Win With Canvas

Build a perfect Post-Service Follow-Up Email in Canvas. Tweak it until it sounds exactly like you. Save the prompt. Now anyone on your team can send a professional follow-up in 30 seconds. Zero mistakes.

Connect Google Drive — The #1 Move for Business Owners
This is the highest-value connection for most small business owners. It takes 3 minutes and pays off every single day after that. Here's exactly how:
1
Click "Apps" in the Left Sidebar
Open ChatGPT on desktop. On the left side, click Apps. This is where all your connections live.
2
Find Google Drive and Click Connect
Scroll through the Apps list and find Google Drive. Click Connect. Sign in with your Google account and approve the connection.
3
Start a New Chat and Click the + Icon
In your message box, click the + icon. You'll now see your connected Google Drive as an option. Select a file to bring it into the conversation.
4
Share a File and Ask Questions
Pick any file from your Drive and ask ChatGPT about it. Example: "Using my pricing guide, write a proposal for a new client who needs [service]."
💡 Real Business Win With Google Drive

Store your price list in Google Drive. Connect it once. Now anytime you need a quote, ask ChatGPT and it pulls from your real pricing — no more manually looking things up. No more math errors. No more time wasted.
